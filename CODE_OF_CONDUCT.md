# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

This project exists to help people prepare for interviews and do their jobs better. Many of the people reading it are early in their careers, interviewing under pressure, or learning in a second language. That shapes the standard we hold ourselves to.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- **Correcting content, not people.** "This answer is out of date - `kubectl rollout undo` no longer behaves this way" is a contribution. "Anyone who wrote this has never touched production" is not.
- Giving concrete, actionable feedback in reviews, with a source or a command that demonstrates the point.
- Assuming a contributor is doing their best with the knowledge they have, and remembering that an interview guide is read by beginners.
- Accepting that reasonable engineers disagree - many answers here have legitimate alternatives, and "it depends, and here is what it depends on" is often the better answer.
- Focusing on what is best for readers preparing for real interviews.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments; public or private.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberately derailing review discussions.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information - including the **names, contact details, or identifying descriptions of interviewers, recruiters, or candidates** - without their explicit permission.
- Gatekeeping: mocking a question as "too basic", or dismissing a contributor's experience level, region, or employer.
- Publishing content the contributor is not free to share (see below).

## Project-specific expectations

Because this repository collects real interview experience, three rules go beyond the usual:

1. **Share questions, never people.** Contribute the technical substance of what was asked. Do not name the interviewer, describe them, quote private messages, or include screenshots of scheduling tools, emails, or chats.
2. **Respect confidentiality obligations.** Do not post content covered by an NDA, take-home assignments or their solutions, proprietary architecture diagrams, internal documents, or anything that would identify a specific unreleased system. If you are unsure whether you may share something, do not.
3. **No plagiarism.** Answers must be written in your own words. Do not paste vendor documentation, books, paid courses, or another repository's content without permission and attribution. Cite sources with links rather than copying them.

Reports of companies and their interview rounds are welcome; complaints about individuals are not the purpose of this project. Keep write-ups factual and free of grievance - "the round was three hours with no break" is useful information, an attack on a named engineer is not.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces - issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and code review - and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the maintainers privately:

- Open a **private report** through the repository's **Security** tab → _Report a vulnerability_ (GitHub's private reporting form works for conduct reports too and is not publicly visible), or
- Contact the maintainer directly on GitHub: [@mchittineni](https://github.com/mchittineni).

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident. Reports concerning a maintainer will be handled by another maintainer where one is available.

If content about you was published here without your consent - your name, your words, or details identifying you as an interviewer or candidate - say so in a report and it will be removed promptly, ahead of any other discussion.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will follow these consequences for any action judged to be in violation:

| Level                | Community impact                                                                   | Consequence                                                                                                              |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **1. Correction**    | Inappropriate language, or other behaviour deemed unprofessional or unwelcome.     | A private written warning, clarity on why the behaviour was inappropriate, and possibly a request for a public apology.  |
| **2. Warning**       | A single incident, or a series of actions.                                         | A warning with consequences for continued behaviour, and no interaction with the people involved for a specified period. |
| **3. Temporary ban** | A serious violation, including sustained inappropriate behaviour.                  | A temporary ban from any interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period.                  |
| **4. Permanent ban** | A pattern of violation, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward a group. | A permanent ban from any public interaction within the project community.                                                |

Content that violates the confidentiality or privacy rules above may be removed immediately, without waiting for the discussion to conclude.
